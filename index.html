ABOUT THE PROPOSED KOLLEG

A shared response to interconnected challenges.

The future of biodiversity, climate resilience and food security cannot be addressed in isolation.

The proposed Akure Humboldt Kolleg 2027 is conceived as a meeting point for researchers, innovators, practitioners, policy voices and communities to exchange evidence and explore practical responses to complex environmental and food-system challenges.

Across five intersecting themes, the conversation will focus on linking science with implementation, innovation with inclusion and research with real-world outcomes.

01Connect disciplinesAcross science, society and policy
02Advance solutionsThrough innovation and local knowledge
03Build partnershipsFor sustainable, measurable impact
